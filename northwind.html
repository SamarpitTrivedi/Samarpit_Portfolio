Northwind Business Analytics

A Power BI analytics project for the Northwind trading dataset, turning sales, customer, supplier, operations, and employee data into business-ready reporting.

View Presentation View GitHub
FocusSales performance, customer trends, supplier contribution, operations, and employee productivity.
MethodsPower BI modeling, KPI design, interactive dashboards, and business reporting.
OutputA multi-page business report that helps stakeholders monitor performance and spot improvement areas.

Objective

Translate trading data into decisions.

The project analyzes the Northwind business dataset to create a clear management view of revenue, order activity, customer behavior, supplier performance, operational movement, and employee contribution.

Business Questions

  • Which products, customers, and regions contribute most to sales?
  • How do suppliers and categories influence business performance?
  • Where can operations and employee performance be monitored more effectively?

Methodology

  • Structured the Northwind data into analysis-ready views.
  • Designed Power BI report pages around core business functions.
  • Built KPIs and visuals for performance monitoring.
  • Presented findings through a business report PDF.

Analysis Areas

  • Sales and order performance trends.
  • Customer segments and purchasing behavior.
  • Supplier contribution and product movement.
  • Operations and employee-level reporting.
