Customer Churn Prediction

A machine learning project built to identify telecom customers at risk of churning so businesses can act earlier and improve retention.

View GitHub Back to Projects
Dataset7,000+ telecom customer records with nearly 40 features.
Best ModelXGBClassifier with approximately 82.7 percent accuracy.
GoalHelp businesses proactively engage customers before they leave.

Objective

Predict which customers are likely to churn.

The project focuses on building a model that helps a telecom business identify high-risk customers and improve retention through timely intervention.

Challenges

Feature-heavy data and meaningful model selection.

  • Understanding which demographic and service attributes influence churn most.
  • Preparing categorical features through encoding and transformation.
  • Choosing a model accurate enough to support real business decisions.

Methodology

  • Exploratory analysis and preprocessing.
  • Feature scaling with MinMaxScaler.
  • Model testing across Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Decision Trees, and XGBoost.
  • Evaluation on unseen data for accuracy and classification quality.

Analysis

  • Demographics such as age and marital status showed relevant churn signals.
  • Contract and service choices were strong predictors of retention behavior.
  • XGBClassifier performed best on the test set.
