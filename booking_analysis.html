Business Analysis

Hotel Booking Analysis

A booking channel analysis project aimed at uncovering hotel performance trends, cancellation drivers, and opportunities to improve retention and profitability.

View Presentation View GitHub
FocusChannel performance, cancellations, and booking value trends.
MethodsData cleaning, EDA, transformation, visualization, and reporting.
OutputClear recommendations for hotel retention and marketing strategy.

Objective

Turn hotel booking data into strategic decisions.

The analysis looks at how channels, room types, property ratings, and seasonality shape booking behavior, cancellations, and revenue performance.

Business Questions

  • Which booking channels perform best?
  • What factors contribute most to cancellation rates?
  • How do property types, lead times, and booking values differ?

Methodology

  • Data cleaning and preparation.
  • Exploratory data analysis.
  • Data wrangling and transformation.
  • Visualization and report building.

Analysis

  • Booking trends and channel performance patterns.
  • Cancellation drivers and lead-time effects.
  • Root causes behind profitability and conversion disparities.
