Booking Channel Analysis

Objective and Problem Statement

The main objective of this analysis is to apply data analysis techniques to a sales dataset to find key business insights, identify trends, and recommend actionable strategies

*Identify Key Observations: Determine significant trends and patterns in hotel booking data, understand how booking behaviors differ across various channels, room types, and star ratings, and find insights into customer cancellation behavior.

*Root Cause Analysis: Investigate the factors that lead to high cancellation rates, explain why some booking channels or property types perform better than others, and see if booking values and stay durations are influenced by seasonal or time-based trends.

METHODOLOGY

Our methodology followed these steps:

  • Data Cleaning & Preparation
  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)s
  • Data Wrangling & Transformation
  • Visualization & Reporting

ANALYSIS

We identified the following trends and patterns:

  • Booking Trends & Channel Performance
  • Cancellation Drivers
  • Root Causes for Disparity
