Zomato Analysis

A Power BI analysis project focused on restaurant performance, cuisine trends, ratings, and regional patterns inside Zomato data.

View GitHub Back to Projects
PlatformPower BI analysis built on cleaned and modeled restaurant data.
FocusRestaurant counts, cuisines, ratings, costs, and regional trends.
OutcomeInteractive reporting for clearer restaurant and cuisine performance insight.

Concept

Restaurant analytics built for operational clarity.

The project transforms a public Zomato dataset into a Power BI reporting system that helps stakeholders understand restaurant patterns, cuisine distribution, and rating behavior.

Approach

From broad visuals to deeper restaurant and cuisine insights.

The workflow moved from basic country-level counts toward more analytical segmentation by restaurant offerings, customer ratings, and overall service mix.

Techniques Used

  • Power Query for cleaning and transformation.
  • Star-schema modeling for structured analysis.
  • DAX KPIs for total restaurants, cities, cuisines, ratings, and cost metrics.

Business Value

  • Understand cuisine and restaurant concentration by region.
  • Track ratings and cost behavior at a usable summary level.
  • Support better restaurant and platform decision-making.
