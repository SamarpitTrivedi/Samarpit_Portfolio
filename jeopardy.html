Data Analytics

Retail Sales Analytics

An interactive business intelligence project focused on sales performance, channel trends, product movement, and customer behavior across retail operations.

View Visualization Back to Projects
Revenue Total revenue analyzed: $2.8M across online and offline retail channels.
Customers 18,394 active customers with clear signals around payment and purchase behavior.
Insight Strong category concentration with specific products driving most of the value.

Concept

A dashboard system designed for clear commercial storytelling.

The project combines KPI tracking, time-series trends, and category-level performance to give a fuller view of business health across products, revenue, profit, and sales channels.

Data

Integrated retail metrics across channels and products.

  • Daily revenue comparison across online and offline channels.
  • Product-level sales, profit, growth, and category performance.
  • Payment behavior, refund rate, and active customer base analysis.

Key Findings

What stood out during analysis.

  • Electronics dominate revenue, with iPhone 14 Pro contributing a significant share.
  • Digital payments account for most transactions.
  • Most business metrics show positive month-over-month movement, while a few products trend downward.

Process

How the insights were surfaced.

  • Revenue trend dashboards.
  • Financial KPI dashboards.
  • Product performance and category analysis views.
